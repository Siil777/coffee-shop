About the developer

Hello! I'm Oliver Cassius, a software developer with a passion for real world projects. Based in Estonia, I blend technical expertise with creative vision to bring ideas to life through code and imagery.

My journey began over a five ago, when I discovered the power of programming to solve problems and create engaging experiences. Since then, I've honed my skills in various programming languages and frameworks, always eager to learn and adapt to new technologies.

Beyond coding, I have a keen interest in many subjects like art, music, and nature. These passions fuel my creativity and inspire me to approach development with a unique perspective.
