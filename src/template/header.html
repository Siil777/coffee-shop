About the Artist

Hello! I'm Alex, a photographer based in the Pacific Northwest with a passion for capturing the raw, untamed beauty of the natural world and the nuanced complexity of human emotion.

My journey in photography began over a decade ago, fueled by a simple desire to freeze fleeting moments in time. Today, I specialize in landscape and portraiture, striving to infuse every shot with depth and narrative.

When I'm not behind the lens, you can find me hiking remote trails or enjoying a quiet cup of coffee. Let's connect and create something beautiful together.
